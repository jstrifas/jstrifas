## 🧠 How I Work

I replace specs with working prototypes. When I see a product problem, I build the smallest version that proves the idea, put it in front of real users, and let their reaction drive what gets built next.

That means every project here started with a question: can we solve this faster, more simply, and in a way a non-technical user would actually trust? I pick the stack that gets to a real answer quickly — then ship it.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

- **LotPrice** — AI pricing tool for independent car dealers &nbsp; [![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-0E7C61?style=flat-square)](https://vehicle-inventory-price-tool.vercel.app/) &nbsp; [Source](https://github.com/jstrifas/Vehicle-Inventory-Price-Tool)

  60,000 independent car dealers in the US price their inventory by gut feel and what they paid for it at auction. The result: cars sitting on lots for 45, 60, 90 days while the same vehicle sells down the road for $1,500 less. The dealers who need pricing help the most are the least equipped to act on data — they are on the lot between customers, not in front of a computer.

  LotPrice answers one question: is this car priced right, and if not, what should I do? It shows a recommended price backed by real comparable sales from nearby dealerships, a plain-language AI explanation of exactly why, and how long the car should take to sell. No charts, no dashboards, no jargon. One number, one action.

  Demoed live for a CPO and CTO of a leading dealership SaaS platform. CTO reaction: "This is exactly how we already think about dealer inventory health."

  Built with Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and the Anthropic Claude API.

- **Clinical Transcript Navigator** — AI-powered care routing for specialty healthcare &nbsp; [![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-0E7C61?style=flat-square)](https://clinical-transcript-navigator.vercel.app/) &nbsp; [Source](https://github.com/jstrifas/Clinical-Transcript-Navigator)

  Specialty care coordination is broken. Patients get referred to a specialist and fall through the cracks — they never schedule, cannot navigate the authorization process, or give up when a care team fails to route them correctly. For employers paying for value-based care, every failed navigation is both a clinical failure and a cost problem.

  The Clinical Transcript Navigator extracts structured clinical facts from unstructured patient call transcripts using Claude, maps them against clinical standard operating procedures via a rule engine, and generates prioritized routing recommendations for care coordinators. A cross-model evaluator validates outputs before they reach a care team.

  Built with Streamlit, Python, and the Anthropic Claude API.

## 🏢 Products Shipped to Market

- **Mission Control** — Cloud cost optimization platform, built 0 to 1 &nbsp; [![Case Study](https://img.shields.io/badge/Case_Study-1B3252?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/jstrifas/Mission-Control/blob/main/README.md) &nbsp; [Full Story](https://johnstrifas.com/work)

  Mission Cloud's customers were paying for AWS managed services and getting a reactive relationship: submit a ticket, wait for a response. With no visibility into their cloud spend and no proactive guidance, churn was running at 8% and accelerating.

  I founded the PM function and built Mission Control from zero — a SaaS platform that gave customers a real-time health score across cost, reliability, performance, and security, a two-way recommendation engine surfacing specific savings opportunities with step-by-step remediation, and complete visibility into every AWS resource under management.

  Results: 22% average reduction in AWS spend per customer, 92% CSAT, 80% retention of at-risk accounts, full reversal of the 8% churn trend. Launched on AWS Marketplace. Demoed at AWS re:Invent.

  No code to show — this was enterprise SaaS built with a team of engineers I partnered with as the founding PM. Screenshots and the full product story live in the case study above.
