That means every project here started with a question: can we solve this faster, more simply, and in a way a non-technical user would actually trust? I pick the stack that gets to a real answer quickly — then ship it.

🚀 Featured Projects
LotPrice — AI pricing tool for independent car dealers   ![Live Demo](https://vehicle-inventory-price-tool.vercel.app/)   Source
60,000 independent car dealers in the US price their inventory by gut feel and what they paid for it at auction. The result: cars sitting on lots for 45, 60, 90 days while the same vehicle sells down the road for $1,500 less. The dealers who need pricing help the most are the least equipped to act on data — they are on the lot between customers, not in front of a computer analyzing spreadsheets.
LotPrice answers one question for that dealer: is this car priced right, and if not, what should I do? It shows a recommended price backed by real comparable sales from nearby dealerships, a plain-language AI explanation of exactly why, and how long the car should take to sell at that price. No charts, no dashboards, no jargon. One number, one action, five minutes between customers.
Demoed live in a final round interview with a CPO and CTO of a leading dealership SaaS platform. The CTO's exact reaction: "This is exactly how we already think about dealer inventory health."
Built with Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and the Anthropic Claude API.

Clinical Transcript Navigator — AI-powered care routing for specialty healthcare   ![Live Demo](https://clinical-transcript-navigator.vercel.app/)   Source
Specialty care coordination is broken. Patients get referred to a specialist and fall through the cracks — they never schedule, cannot navigate the authorization process, or give up when a care team fails to route them correctly. For employers paying for value-based care, every failed navigation is both a clinical failure and a cost problem.
The Clinical Transcript Navigator extracts structured clinical facts from unstructured patient call transcripts using Claude, maps them against clinical standard operating procedures via a rule engine, and generates prioritized routing recommendations for care coordinators. A cross-model evaluator validates outputs before they reach a care team, adding the human-in-the-loop layer that healthcare AI requires.
Built with Streamlit, Python, and the Anthropic Claude API.

Mission Control — Cloud cost optimization platform, 0 to 1   Case Study   Full Story
AWS managed services customers had no way to see where they stood on their cloud spend, no proactive recommendations, and no strategic value from their managed services relationship beyond ticket resolution. Churn was running at 8% and accelerating.
I founded the PM function at Mission Cloud and built Mission Control from zero: a cloud cost optimization SaaS that turned a reactive ticketing tool into a proactive intelligence platform. The product gave customers a real-time health score for their AWS environment, cost benchmarks against the AWS Well-Architected Framework, and a two-way recommendation engine that surfaced savings opportunities before customers had to ask.
The results: 22% average reduction in AWS spend per customer, 92% CSAT, 80% retention of at-risk accounts, and a full reversal of the 8% churn trend. Launched on AWS Marketplace and demoed at AWS re:Invent.
No code to show here — this was enterprise SaaS built by a team of engineers I partnered with. The full product story with screenshots lives at johnstrifas.com/work.
